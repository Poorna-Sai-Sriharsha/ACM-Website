AI Innovators Arena

AI with Prompt Challenge

Organized by ACM Student Chapter · R.V.R. & J.C. College of Engineering, Guntur

Category Prompt Quest
Team Size 2 Members per Team
Rounds 2 — AI Quiz + Prompting Challenge
Round 1 Duration 10 Minutes
Round 2 Duration 90–180 Minutes
Mode Offline
Difficulty Beginner to Intermediate

About the Event

The preliminary round consists of an objective quiz comprising 15 MCQs designed to assess participants' fundamental knowledge of Artificial Intelligence. Qualified teams will then be given three AI-based challenges simultaneously and must complete them within the allotted time. The challenges assess creativity, prompt engineering, AI-assisted development, and problem-solving skills.

Round 2 Tasks

  • Image Reverse Engineering — Recreate a given image using AI tools through effective prompt engineering.
  • AI-Assisted Website Development — Build a functional website based on the provided problem statement and requirements using AI tools.
  • LLM Model Building — Develop an LLM-based solution according to the given requirements.

Round 1 — AI Quiz

The preliminary round consists of an objective quiz covering the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI.

Duration: 10 Minutes    Questions: 15 MCQs

Topics Covered

  • Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals
  • Machine Learning Basics
  • Generative AI
  • Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Prompt Engineering
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • Computer Vision
  • AI Tools & Platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, etc.)
  • AI Ethics & Responsible AI
  • Real-World Applications of AI

Qualification: The highest-scoring teams will advance to the Prompting Challenge.

Round 2 — Prompting Challenge

Qualified teams will be given three AI-based challenges simultaneously and must complete them within the allotted time.

Duration: 90–180 Minutes

Evaluation Criteria

  • Accuracy
  • Creativity
  • Implementation Quality
  • Functionality
  • Efficient Use of AI Tools
  • Problem-Solving Approach

Rules & Regulations

  1. Participation is allowed only in teams of two members.
  2. Participants must carry a valid college ID card for verification.
  3. Participants must provide valid contact details during registration. The organizers are not responsible for missed communications due to incorrect or invalid information.
  4. Each team will be provided with one computer system for the duration of the event.
  5. Teams must report to the venue at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.
  6. Only teams qualifying in the Prelims are eligible to participate in the Mains.
  7. During the Prelims, the use of mobile phones, smart devices, external AI tools, and internet browsing is strictly prohibited.
  8. During the Mains, internet access is permitted only for the designated AI tools specified by the organizers.
  9. The use of mobile phones, personal messaging, communication with other teams, or sharing prompts and code during the Mains is strictly prohibited.
  10. Plagiarism, the use of unauthorized pre-built templates, external assistance, impersonation, or any unfair means will result in immediate disqualification.
  11. Teams will be evaluated based on prompt quality, visual accuracy, UI/UX, functional completeness, and the logical correctness of AI-generated responses, as applicable to the challenge.
  12. All required submissions, including prompts, generated outputs, source code, web links, and response logs, must be submitted through the designated portal before the time limit expires.
  13. In the event of a tie, the team with the higher evaluation score will be ranked higher. If the tie persists, the team with the earliest valid submission will be given preference.
  14. The decision of the judges and the organizing committee shall be final and binding.
