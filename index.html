About the ACM Student Chapter

ℹ About ACM

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) Student Chapter of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, R.V.R. & J.C. College of Engineering (Autonomous), brings together students and faculty who share a passion for computing. As the student wing of the world's largest educational and scientific computing society, the chapter works to extend learning beyond the classroom through technical talks, hands-on workshops, coding contests, research symposiums and industry interactions.

The chapter is affiliated with Acharya Nagarjuna University and functions under the guidance of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, with active participation from students across all years of study.

🎯 Our Vision

To nurture a vibrant community of computing professionals and researchers who advance the discipline of computer science through innovation, ethical practice, and lifelong learning — empowering students to become globally competent computing leaders.

🛠 Our Mission

To provide a platform for students to explore emerging technologies, sharpen problem-solving skills, engage in collaborative research, and build a strong professional network — bridging the gap between academic learning and industry-relevant expertise.
