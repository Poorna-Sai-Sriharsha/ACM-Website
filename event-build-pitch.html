About the Event

Build & Pitch is a system design challenge that tests participants' ability to analyse a real-world problem, design a scalable software solution, develop a prototype, and effectively communicate their engineering decisions.

Think. Design. Prototype. Pitch.

Eligibility

  • Team size: exactly 3 members.
  • Participants can be from any department and any college.
  • Cross-department and cross-college teams are permitted.
  • One participant can be part of only one team.

Round 1 — Preliminary Round

The preliminary round consists of MCQs based on System Design concepts.

Format: 20 MCQs    Duration: 20 Minutes

Date: 12 August    Time: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM    Venue: Lab (to be confirmed)

Round 2 — Build & Pitch (Main Round)

Date: 12 August 2026    Time: 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM    Duration: 3 Hours

Participants are not expected to build a production-ready application. Instead, they are expected to analyse the problem, design an appropriate solution, develop a functional prototype, and justify their engineering decisions.

Problem Statement

At the beginning of the round, every team will receive a problem statement, functional requirements, constraints, expected deliverables, and judging criteria. The problem statement will only be revealed at the start of the Main Round.

Typical Constraints

  • User scale
  • Budget limitations
  • Performance requirements
  • Security requirements
  • Availability requirements

Expected Workflow

1. Understand the Problem

Each team will be provided with a Design Canvas (worksheet) to document their initial design. The canvas should include: Functional Requirements, Non-functional Requirements, Assumptions, User Flow, High-Level Architecture, Database Entities, API List (Basic), and Technology Stack.

2. Prototype Development

Teams should develop a prototype that demonstrates the proposed solution. The objective is to demonstrate the workflow and core functionality rather than complete implementation.

3. Presentation Preparation

Teams should prepare a presentation that clearly explains their solution, design decisions, prototype, and overall system architecture.

Deliverables

  1. Presentation (PPT) — Mandatory
  2. Prototype — Any working prototype
  3. Architecture Diagram — Mandatory
  4. Database Design — ER Diagram or Schema
  5. API List — Basic endpoint definitions

PPT Structure (Max 10 Slides)

  • Slide 1 — Problem Statement & Objectives
  • Slide 2 — Functional & Non-functional Requirements, Assumptions
  • Slide 3 — High-Level Design (Architecture Diagram, Components, Data Flow)
  • Slide 4 — Database Design (ER Diagram, Tables, Relationships)
  • Slide 5 — API Design (Important Endpoints)
  • Slide 6 — Prototype (Main Screens, User Flow)
  • Slide 7 — Scalability & Security
  • Slide 8 — Trade-offs & Challenges
  • Slide 9 — Future Improvements
  • Slide 10 — Live Demo

Allowed Resources

  • AI Tools: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, GitHub Copilot
  • Search: Google, Stack Overflow
  • Design: Figma, Canva, Draw.io, Excalidraw
  • Open-source libraries, VS Code, any programming language or framework

AI Usage Policy

AI tools are permitted. However, teams must be able to explain every design decision. Blindly copying AI-generated solutions without understanding them may lead to lower scores during questioning. Judges may ask any member of the team to justify the architecture, APIs, or technology choices.

Judging Criteria

  • Problem Understanding
  • System Design
  • Prototype
  • Innovation
  • Presentation
  • Q&A

General Rules

  1. Each team must consist of exactly 3 members.
  2. One or more desktop systems will be provided. Participants are not required to bring their own laptops.
  3. Free internet access will be available throughout the event.
  4. Use of AI tools is permitted, but all design decisions must be justified during evaluation.
  5. The problem statement will be disclosed only at the start of the Main Round.
  6. Teams are expected to create original solutions during the event. Pre-built solutions and existing GitHub repos for the same problem are not permitted.
  7. PPT submission and optional supporting links will be collected after the event through a Google Form shared by the organizers.
  8. Judges' decisions will be final and binding.
  9. Any form of plagiarism or unfair means may lead to disqualification.
