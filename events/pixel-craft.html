Pixel Craft

UI/UX Design Challenge

Organized by ACM Student Chapter · R.V.R. & J.C. College of Engineering, Guntur

✦ Design. Prototype. Present. ✦
CategoryUI/UX Design
Team Size2 Members per Team
PrelimsUI/UX Quiz — 11th August 2026, 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
MainsUI/UX Design Sprint — 11th August 2026, 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
VenueNC1 Lab

About the Event

Pixel Craft is a UI/UX design competition that tests participant's creativity, design thinking, and problem-solving skills. The event consists of a preliminary quiz followed by a Design Sprint, where participants create a UI/UX solution for a given real-world problem.

Key Event Highlights

  • Prelims Screening: 30 MCQs assessing design fundamentals, color theory, wireframing & Figma basics.
  • Mains Design Sprint: Live 3-hour prototyping challenge based on an unannounced real-world problem statement.
  • Prototyping Tool: Craft high-fidelity interactive prototypes exclusively in Figma.
  • Live Jury Pitch: Present design concepts, navigation user flows, and UX decisions directly to judges.

Prelims — UI/UX Quiz

The preliminary round is designed to assess participant's understanding of fundamental UI/UX concepts.

Questions: 30 MCQs    Qualification: Top-performing teams advance to Mains

Topics Covered

  • UI & UX Basics
  • Design Principles
  • Color Theory
  • Typography
  • Wireframing & Prototyping
  • Accessibility
  • Figma Basics

Mains — Design Sprint

Qualified participants will be provided with a real-world problem statement by the organizers. Based on the given problem, participants must design a UI/UX prototype using Figma.

Participants will present their design using the Figma prototype and briefly explain the user flow and design approach to the judges.

Focus Topics & Key Deliverables

  • High-Fidelity Wireframes & Visual Design
  • User Experience (UX) Flow & Navigation Mapping
  • Interactive Prototyping in Figma
  • Design System Consistency (Typography, Color Palette, Spacing)
  • Final Design Presentation & Defense

Expected Solution

Participants are expected to design a UI/UX prototype using Figma that effectively addresses the given problem statement. The prototype should clearly demonstrate the proposed solution and its user flow. Participants may be asked to briefly explain their design and navigation during the presentation.

Rules & Regulations

  1. Participation should be a team of 2 members.
  2. Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  3. The quiz consists of 30 MCQs, and the top-performing teams will qualify for Mains.
  4. The problem statement for the Design Sprint will be announced only at the beginning of the round.
  5. The UI/UX prototype must be created entirely during the competition using prototyping tools and AI.
  6. Participants should present their design and briefly explain the user flow and design decisions.
  7. All submitted work must be original. Any form of plagiarism or unfair practice will lead to disqualification.
